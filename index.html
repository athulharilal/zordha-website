Technical Reference

Surface finishes & formats.

Granite surface characteristics can be tailored based on interior or exterior architectural requirements.

Available Surface Finishes

Tactile and visual surface treatments available for Indian granite:

Polished High specular gloss with rich colour depth. Recommended for countertops, vanities, and interior cladding.
Honed Smooth, non-reflective matte satin surface. Ideal for low-glare flooring, walls, and contemporary spaces.
Flamed High-temperature textured thermal finish. Provides slip resistance for exterior paving and walkways.
Leathered Subtle undulating tactile texture with a satin sheen. Resists fingerprints and highlights natural stone veins.
Bush-Hammered Uniform mechanical pitting for heavy-duty outdoor grip and architectural facades.

Formats & Thickness Standards

Export dimensions and standard thickness specifications:

Gangsaw Slabs Approx. 2800+ mm length × 1600+ mm height. Ideal for large seamless countertops and full-height cladding.
Cutter Slabs Approx. 1800+ mm length × 650+ mm height. Economical format for stairs, sills, and cut pieces.
Calibrated Tiles Standard dimensions (300×300, 600×300, 600×600 mm) for precision flooring and walling.
Standard Thickness 20 mm (±1mm) and 30 mm (±1mm) standard export. 40+ mm available for custom architectural projects.
